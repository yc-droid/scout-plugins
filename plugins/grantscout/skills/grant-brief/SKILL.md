---
name: grant-brief
description: Turn one federal grant opportunity into an eligibility checklist and application plan. Use when the user names a grant or opportunity number, or asks whether they qualify for a grant, what it requires or how to apply.
---

To brief one grant:

1. Call `get_grant_details` with the opportunity ID, number or Grants.gov link.
2. Summarize in plain language: what it funds, who can apply, award floor and ceiling, number of awards, cost-sharing, and key dates (or forecast dates).
3. Build an eligibility checklist from the eligibility text, phrased as yes/no questions the user can answer.
4. Draft a back-planned timeline from the close date: registrations (SAM.gov and Grants.gov registration can take weeks), narrative, budget, letters and internal review.
5. List the agency contact and the official link. Don't write or submit the application unless the user asks for help drafting a section.
